Mobility · Time series · Python · GCP · BigQuery

EV battery performance analysis

Electric vehicles are a cornerstone of cleaner transport globally. In East Africa, electric motorcycles (boda bodas) are especially promising as public transport, carrying passengers across both busy urban centres and rural areas where other options are limited. Their climate benefit, however, depends on how sustainable they are, i.e. how long the batteries last and how well fleets are run.

I analysed battery swap and telemetry data to understand how batteries degrade with use, then built time series forecasts of fleet usage. findings on degradation patterns and usage trends show how operators could plan swap capacity, maintenance, and replacement more effectively. Longer battery life means fewer batteries manufactured and discarded, and better-planned swapping means less idle infrastructure, which makes electric mobility more economical and more sustainable.

Healthcare · Python

Mapping healthcare access gaps

Access to care varies widely across Nairobi, and averages hide where the shortfalls are. This project compares sub-counties on facility access, bed availability, and selected health services, measured against population size and recommended target ratios.

The analysis highlights which areas are underserved and by how much several sub-counties fall below the UN Department of Economic and Social Affairs SDG-recommended targets, giving planners and health organisations an evidence base for prioritising resources.

Operations · SQL · Python · Excel

Team Performance & Insights

I analysed data from roughly 170,000 cases to assess an operations team’s performance against its “instant service” promise. Using SQL, I cleaned inconsistent timestamps, then measured backlog age, time to first review, resolution time, reopen rates and agent throughput, and presented the results as a deck. Cases entered the backlog within 45 seconds and reviews took about 11 minutes, yet cases waited 18 hours for a first review, driving a 72-hour average resolution time. The reopen rate was 31%, and resolution time worsened over time even as the resolution rate improved.

My analysis identified six cases with 200+ hour backlog delays and recommended fixing the first-review delay to improve resolution time and reduce reopen rates.

iGaming · Tableau

iGaming Dashboard

I built an interactive Tableau dashboard for an iGaming operator, tracking active players, deposits and net gaming revenue (NGR) from February to April, alongside the registration-to-deposit funnel, revenue by product and performance by market.

Active players grew from 3,663 to 4,092 and deposits rose to €2.27M, but NGR fell in April to €586K. Casino generated about 75% of revenue, and Sweden, Finland and Norway led the markets. Bonus spend was positively correlated with NGR, turnover and active players. I also flagged Malta and Hungary as loss-making markets with growth potential.

Mobility · Looker · GCP

Ride-Hailing Dashboard

I built an interactive Looker Studio dashboard analysing ride-hailing supply against demand for the week of 7–13 November 2016. It tracks searches, zero-car searches, coverage ratio and driver hours, with an hourly coverage heatmap and filters for day, hour and coverage level.

Of 7,047 searches, 1,858 (26.4%) found no car, giving an average coverage of 74%. Coverage was weakest in the early hours and dipped on 11 November, when searches peaked. Only 24% of online hours (21,288 of 88,656) included a booking, which suggests a mismatch between when and where drivers are online and when demand occurs, rather than a simple shortage of drivers.

Actuarial · Python

Claims reserving with chain ladder

Insurers need to estimate what claims will ultimately cost before they are fully settled. Using payment data, I built a claims development triangle, calculated loss development factors, and projected ultimate losses with the chain ladder method. The project walks through each step of the reserving process, from data preparation to the final reserve estimate, in clear, reproducible code.

Sports · ML · Python · SQL

Football match outcome predictor

A web app that estimates match outcomes across leagues and teams. Instead of treating all history equally, the model uses recency-weighted performance, so recent form counts for more than results from seasons ago. It shows an end-to-end workflow from data and modelling through to a usable interface.
