About

I’m a Nairobi-based data scientist with experience spanning freelance analytics, actuarial consulting, insurance finance, and research. I have worked with teams at Pula Advisors, Lepton Actuarial & Consulting, UAP Old Mutual, and InfoTrak Research.

My actuarial background shapes how I approach data science: define the question, validate the inputs, test the model, and explain the result in terms people can use. I enjoy collaborating with cross-functional teams and translating technical findings into clear next steps.

I hold a Bachelor of Science in Actuarial Science from Dedan Kimathi University of Technology and completed Data Science and Machine Learning studies with WorldQuant University.
